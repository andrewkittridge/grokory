## Thesis (after using the live site)

Wave 2 made the board honest and quieter. Using it today: **n=2 still feels like a landing page with a scoreboard attached** — hero + mascot eat the first glance; Hot/Top/New and open seats still whisper “directory” when the truth is two listings. Density (list → stay accurate → share) still beats chrome theater. Thin + premium > thick + hollow.

Bar for every bet: does it make someone **want to list** or **want to Add** on what is actually there?

## Ship-now visual bets (ranked for Andrew pick)

### 1. Scoreboard-up — quieter hero (highest leverage)
**Feel pain:** first screen is brand theater; the two real listings sit below the fold / under a tall hero.
- Shorten hero: keep one-line promise + **one** Share primary + Browse; shrink or tuck mascot.
- Pull the ranked board (or a compact “2 listed” strip) into the first glance.
- Agent “HAVE YOUR BOT LIST IT” stays once, compact — don’t grow it.

### 2. #1-only sunset / spark (desktop leftover)
**Feel pain:** both 01 and 02 can read warm on desktop; premium boards crown one.
- Sunset / spark on **#1 only**; #2+ muted ranks (mobile already closer).
- Hairlines + breathing room held from wave 2.

### 3. One board sort until density
**Feel pain:** Hot / Top / New at n=2 is founding noise.
- Default one sort (Hot or Top); hide or collapse tabs until ~8–12 listings.
- Don’t invent discovery chrome or Featured weight.

### 4. Footer + upload hush
**Feel pain:** footer “SHARE A BOT” + possible Turnstile weight still compete with the one Share hero.
- Drop or further mute footer SHARE text.
- Live Turnstile: interaction-only / quieter placement (wave2 soft note — verify on real `/upload`).

### 5. OPEN seats: invitation, not a third of the board (optional)
**Feel pain:** three quiet “Claim this seat” rows still dominate vertical space vs two live listings.
- Cap visible open seats at **2**, or one “Seats open · Claim” footer under the two rows.
- Keep calm paste invite; no MCP spam return.
