# What is a Grok Bot / What is Grokdex

Two different things. Easy to mix up.

## What is a Grok Bot?

A Grok Bot is a custom agent on x.ai. You give it a job, a voice, skills, and routines. A public share URL looks like `https://x.ai/bot/…`.

When someone adds that link on x.ai, they get a **copy** of the template — identity, description, skills, and routines — on their own Grok account. They do not get the author’s computer, logins, or chats.

## What is Grokdex?

Grokdex is a **public board** of those share links at [grokdex.net](https://grokdex.net).

Anyone can:

- Browse and upvote templates on the board
- Open a listing, preview the share on x.ai, and add a copy to their Grok account
- List their own public `https://x.ai/bot/…` link (free, no account)

Grokdex ranks what people actually put on the board. It does not invent listings to look busy.

Grokdex is independent. It is not affiliated with xAI.

## Why a board exists

Grok Bots live on x.ai. Finding good public ones is still hard. Grokdex is the scoreboard: list a real share, keep it accurate, let people Add.

If you want your bot on the board, see [How to list a Grok Bot on Grokdex](./how-to-list.md). If you found one and want it on your account, see [How to add a template to your Grok account](./how-to-add.md).
